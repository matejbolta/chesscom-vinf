ChessComVINF Privacy Policy

ChessComVINF does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

What the extension does

ChessComVINF runs only on the signed-in Chess.com homepage. It rearranges existing homepage modules, hides modules selected by the user, and adds configurable Quick Play shortcuts that use Chess.com’s own native game-start link.

Data collection

ChessComVINF collects no personal information, account information, authentication information, financial information, location data, browsing history, website content, or user activity. It has no telemetry, analytics, advertising, or developer-operated server.

Local settings

The extension stores only presentation preferences in Chrome’s local extension storage. These preferences include whether VINF is enabled, selected time controls and grid size, native play-panel visibility, homepage-card placement, visibility and order, and Stats visibility, order, and initial expanded or retracted state.

These settings remain in the user’s browser and are not transmitted to the developer. They can be changed from the extension popup or removed by uninstalling the extension and clearing its extension data.

Site access and network activity

The content script is limited to https://www.chess.com/home*. ChessComVINF does not make extension-owned network requests and does not download or execute remote code. Normal requests made by Chess.com remain governed by Chess.com’s own terms and privacy policy.

Third parties

No data is sold or shared with third parties. ChessComVINF is an independent, unofficial extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Chess.com.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes the extension’s data practices, this policy and the Chrome Web Store disclosures will be updated before that version is distributed.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, open an issue in the public GitHub repository.
